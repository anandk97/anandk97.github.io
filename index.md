# Welcome to My Website!

I am an AI/ML Postdoctoral Researcher at **Idaho National Laboratory** in Idaho Falls, ID, focused on physics-informed machine learning and adaptive control. My current work builds physics-informed surrogate models for nuclear reactor simulations in collaboration with NVIDIA, and control algorithms for reactor power regulation and autonomous reactor operation.

I received my Ph.D. in Mechanical Engineering (Advanced Data Science) from the University of Washington in August 2025, advised by Dr. Krithika Manohar. I also hold an M.Sc. from UW, advised by Dr. Steven L. Brunton. My doctoral work spanned digital twins for aircraft manufacturing and real-time ergonomic risk detection, as part of the Boeing Advanced Research Collaboration.

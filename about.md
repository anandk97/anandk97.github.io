### Work Experience

**Idaho National Laboratory**, Idaho Falls, ID  
Modeling and Simulation Scientist (September 2026 – Present) · Postdoctoral Researcher (October 2025 – September 2026)
- **Surrogate modeling for Prometheus (collaboration with NVIDIA):** Developing physics-informed surrogate models with NVIDIA PhysicsNeMo to replace high-fidelity CFD and multiphysics reactor simulations. They reach 99% accuracy with 1–2 second inference, against 1–2 hours per high-fidelity sample. I also built an agentic interface that lets users query reactor simulation results in natural language. This work won 2nd place and the People's Choice award at the INL Research SLAM and was selected for a technical demonstration to the Chairman of the U.S. Nuclear Regulatory Commission.
- **Principal Investigator, Robust Control for Nuclear Power Plants (LDRD 25P1094-008FP):** Leading development of control algorithms for reactor power regulation under parameter uncertainty and limited state observability.
- **IMPACT-AI data assimilation:** Developing a surrogate for a BISON model that runs 10,000× faster, enabling real-time data assimilation from experimental data.
- **Autonomous control for the NRAD TRIGA reactor:** Developing autonomous load-following control algorithms using predictive modeling.

**AI Engineer Intern, Tolstoy AI** (June 2025 – August 2025)
- Built an end-to-end AI pipeline that digitizes public utility records and identifies hazardous lead pipes from unstructured documents, using OCR, deep learning and fuzzy matching.

**Graduate Research Assistant, Boeing Advanced Research Collaboration, University of Washington** (June 2021 – August 2025)
- **Adaptive learning framework for digital twins:** Transfer learning, Gaussian process regression and Kalman filtering to correct model–reality discrepancies and optimize sensor and actuator placement in manufacturing. Recognized with a Research Excellence Award; patent pending.
- **Real-time ergonomic risk detection:** A multimodal sensor-fusion system that combines computer-vision segmentation with gradient boosting and GRU time-series models for real-time risk assessment across 15+ manufacturing technicians. Published in *Nature Communications Engineering*.

**ML Engineering Intern, Mercedes-Benz R&D India** (June 2019 – August 2019)
